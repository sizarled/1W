In short

  • We keep what a membership needs: your account, your viewing codes, your points and what you publish. Nothing more than the pages actually use.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not build advertising profiles from it. There are no third-party trackers on the member pages.
  • Your password is stored only as a hash. Nobody here — including the admins — can read it back.
  • You can change your password, unlink a network and ask for your account to be deleted at any time.
  • An account that is never activated, or that lapses, is deleted after 30 days.

What we collect

What Why
Account — name, username, member ID, email, phone, country, language, avatar and cover So you can sign in, be recognised by other members, and be reached about your account
Security — password hash, session tokens, the IP address and the browser string of each sign-in To check that a session is really yours, and to stop one account being used in several places at once
Viewing codes and devices — the six-digit code, its plan, its age rating, the device name and MAC address A code is bound to one device; that binding is what the code is for
Membership and points — plan, dates, points balance and the ledger of every movement, plus payment references To unlock what has been paid for, and to answer “where did my points go?”
What you do here — likes and dislikes, lists, watch minutes, comments, messages, court cases, notifications and their switches These are the product; a reaction cannot work without being stored
Visitor id — a random six digits, kept in a cookie and in local storage To count a visitor once, and to keep a form from losing its place

We do not collect your address book, your location beyond the country you type, your device's files, or anything from your machine that this site does not show you.

Accounts you link

Signing in with Google, or with any other network once its app is configured, uses that network only to prove who you are. Linking works the same way: you open the network's own window, it asks you to approve, and it sends us back one thing — that you are the account holder.

What arrives from the network

  • An account id for that network, and a display name (on Google, the email address you approved as well).
  • A token, which is kept so the recovery code can be delivered to that network later.

What we never do

  • We never post, message or change anything on your account there.
  • We never read your contacts, your timeline, your photos or your friends.
  • We never receive or store your password for that network — it never leaves it.

One external account belongs to one member. You can remove any link from Account settings → Linked accounts, and the token goes with it. Removing a link does not cancel your membership.

Who else sees it

  • Our database and functions host (Supabase) keeps the rows and runs the checks that sign you in. It is a processor: it does not use your data for anything of its own.
  • The mail script (Google Apps Script) sends the recovery code to your email address. It receives the address and the code, nothing else.
  • The Telegram bot delivers a code or a notice to a chat you linked yourself.
  • The networks you choose — Google, Facebook and the rest — see a sign-in request from this site. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with that.
  • The film and series data comes from TMDB; no member data is sent to it.
  • The activation gate opens a third-party advertising page. That page has its own cookies and its own policy; we only ask it to stay open for the 30 seconds the activation rule needs.

We hand data to a government only when a lawful order names it — and we will say so on this page if it ever happens.

Cookies and local storage

No advertising cookies. These are the keys this site writes, and what each one is for:

Key What it holds
1w.session Your session token — in local storage for 30 days if you ticked “stay signed in”, otherwise in session storage, which the tab drops when it closes
1w.member The name, level and avatar shown in the header, so a page does not flash a blank corner
1w.visitor, cookie 1w_vid The random visitor id, kept two years
1w.geo The country and IP the visitor lookup answered, so it is asked once a day and not once a page
1w.theme Light or dark, so the next page opens in the one you chose
1w.points.seen, 1w.level.seen The last balance and level you saw, so a new one can be announced

Clearing your browser storage signs you out and gives you a new visitor id.

How long we keep it

  • Accounts — an account that never activates, or whose membership lapses and stays lapsed, is deleted after 30 days. Its profile, lists and messages go with it.
  • Payments — kept as a record after the account is gone, but cut loose from it: the row keeps a member reference, not your name, phone or email.
  • Sessions — 12 hours, or 30 days when you asked to stay signed in. Signing out ends it at once, and changing the password ends every other session.
  • Codes — a viewing code lives as long as its plan; the original code is bound to its device and cannot be moved to another one.
  • Visitors — the visitor id expires after two years of no visits.

Your rights

  • See what we hold — most of it is already on your dashboard: profile, codes, devices, sessions, points, notifications and history.
  • Correct it — name, bio, avatar, cover, language and country are editable from your profile. The email on the account is fixed: it is the address the recovery code is bound to, so it is changed by us, by hand, after a request.
  • Keep it out of reach — change the password, sign out every other session, or unlink a network from Account settings.
  • Have it deleted — ask through the contact page; the account is deleted with everything attached to it. Points and paid time are not refunded for a deletion you asked for.
  • Object — write to us and we will explain, or stop, whatever you are objecting to.

Children

The site is for people aged 13 and over. A membership created by someone younger is removed when we learn of it. Titles carry their own age rating, and a viewing code is issued with one.

Changes to this page

When the way we handle something changes, this page changes with it and the date above moves. A change that widens what we collect is announced in the dashboard before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions, a deletion, a correction: use the contact page. Say which member you are (the ID on your dashboard is enough) and what you want done. We answer from the same place a recovery code comes from — the address on your account.
