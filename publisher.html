Publisher

Carry 1World outside the site

A publisher is a creator who works outside 1World: a channel, a newsletter, a group, a blog of their own. Take a blog, a library, a studio or a whole film to your own audience with your own link, and earn points for the people who arrive through it — per sign-up, per activation, per membership.

Three steps, outside the site

  1. 1
    Join the program

    One form: where you publish, from where, and how many people read you. It gives you a seat and a public code, IDPB100001-style.

  2. 2
    Take a link

    One per hub, or one for the whole site. Paste it in your channel, your post, your video description or your newsletter — anywhere outside 1World.

  3. 3
    Get paid in points

    The click is counted for free. A visitor who signs up, activates, or takes a membership through your link pays you — in the same points the site spends.

  • One seat per member; the code is fixed and public.
  • Up to 40 links, one per hub or one for the whole site.
  • One conversion counts once per member per link, per kind.
  • A private or hidden hub cannot be promoted.

Working inside the site is a different thing altogether. A hub an owner lets you into — a blog, a library, a studio — is written in the creator page, which is where the content of a hub is made. This page is for the audience you bring from outside.
